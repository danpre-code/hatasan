BOLOGNA’S FIRST ROBATA

For a taste of authentic Japan, we bring the tradition of Robatayaki to Bologna: an ancient Japanese method of cooking over an open fire. The intense heat of the charcoal sears the surface and seals in flavour, striking the perfect balance between a crisp exterior and a succulent centre.

Robatayaki & Charcoal

The Japanese art of fire

Robatayaki is an ancient charcoal-grilling technique in which fire becomes an instrument of precision. We use carefully selected Japanese charcoal, Binchotan and Ogatan, prized for their clean burn and intense heat.

Fire should bring out the character of an ingredient, allowing its flavour to shine. It is the meeting of fire, ingredients and time.

Our Choice of Wagyu

The finest Japanese beef

Wagyu is one of the finest expressions of Japanese gastronomy. Its distinctive marbling gives it an exceptionally tender texture and a depth of flavour.

Its richness is perfectly suited to Robatayaki: the heat brings out the flavour of the beef as the fat slowly melts. An ingredient that demands one thing above all: precision.

Fish & Seasonality

Following the rhythm of the sea. We choose fish according to the season, working with nature. Whenever possible, we select wild fish at its seasonal best.

Ikejime

Ikejime is an ancient Japanese technique: fish is handled immediately after being caught, allowing greater control over its texture, colour and preservation. The result is firm, clean-textured fish with distinct flavours. A way to respect the ingredient and preserve everything the sea has to offer.

Bluefin Tuna

We select bluefin tuna exclusively from Fuentes and Balfegó, internationally recognised for their quality. Large, mature fish yield Akami, Chutoro and Otoro — the highly prized fatty belly — with exceptional texture and marbling.

ICCAT Traceability

Every fish is certified. The International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas is the international organisation that manages and coordinates conservation and management measures for Atlantic bluefin tuna. The Bluefin Tuna Catch Document system (BCD/eBCD) records and traces the origin of tuna throughout the supply chain, from catch to sale. From sea to plate, every detail matters.

Koshihikari Rice

Balance in every grain

We use only Wadachi-Mai from Niigata. Its natural sweetness and perfect balance of firmness and softness make it ideal for our Nigiri and Uramaki.

Every step receives meticulous attention: washing, cooking, seasoning with our sushi-zu and serving at the right temperature. In sushi, rice is an essential part of the dish.
