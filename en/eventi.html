24 September 2026 · Past event

Bluefin Experience

An Evening of Bluefin Tuna

Akami, Chutoro and Otoro: three expressions of bluefin tuna, explored through cuts, temperatures and textures. With sashimi and nigiri prepared to order, the tasting followed a simple idea: let the ingredient speak for itself.

A selection of sakes accompanied the menu, chosen to complement the progression from clean, lean flavours to richer, more indulgent cuts.

16 July 2026 · Past event

Hata-san × Omakase Guest

The Sea, Unscripted

For one evening, our kitchen welcomed a guest chef for a menu inspired by the day’s catch. With no set sequence announced, each course led naturally to the next, moving between raw fish, light marinades and charcoal grilling.

A dinner in our dining rooms celebrating the pleasure of leaving the choices to the chef — a preview of our future Omakase counter.
